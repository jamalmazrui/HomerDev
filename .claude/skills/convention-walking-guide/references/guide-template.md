# Attendee Guide and Organizer Worksheet

TASKS FIRST, ALWAYS. Every main H2 section is something a traveler sets out to do -- get to the hotel, check in, collect the packet, reach a session, find a restroom, eat, relieve a guide dog -- never a place. A traveler asks "how do I get to lunch?", not "what is on the second floor?", so the guide answers that question where they look for it. A sort by place (floor, wing or room) is allowed only as an appendix that links back to the tasks, never as a second copy of the directions.

Use this outline flexibly; omit empty sections rather than filling them with assumptions.

- H1: Event walking guide.
- Opening: dates, timezone, venue/address, as-of date, direct help number.
- The basics, one short paragraph: one building or several, which floor holds the convention, where the sleeping floors and any tower are, and whether everything is reachable without stairs or going outside. Every route then fills in this map.
- H2 Contents: links to main sections.
- H2 Get to the hotel: H3 transit, rideshare/taxi, and overflow transfer when supported.
- H2 Check in and reach your room: hotel reception, elevators, room-floor orientation.
- H2 Collect your convention packet: distinguish convention registration from hotel check-in.
- H2 Reach general sessions and banquet.
- H2 Find workshops, committees, and gatherings.
- H2 Visit exhibits.
- H2 Find a restroom, use elevators/stairs, or get ice/soda when supported details are available.
- H2 Get meals, drinks, and food to go: seated/counter service, pickup, market, delivery, and supported nearby routes.
- H2 Use gym or other amenities.
- H2 Take your guide dog to relief: supported directions, surface, bins, steps and return access.
- H2 Get assistance or report a change: where people help -- the convention office, the information table, volunteers serving as talking signs -- as well as the help number.
- Optional appendix: compact location index cross-linking to tasks.
- Optional appendix: general discovery/route-learning suggestions.
- Appendix: free mobile tools with platform, cost, practical use, user evidence, limits and sources.
- Do not put an organizer checklist in the attendee guide; use a separate worksheet.
- H2 Sources: current venue evidence separately from historical method examples.

Describe unpublished route geometry as provisional. Publish only the supported portion with a clear starting point; record the connector as an organizer task. Do not fill attendee sections with statements about information not found.

## Organizer worksheet

Include priorities (designated dog relief/return and critical step-free connections first), one record per checked route, source inventory/conflicts, plan revision/identity, map reasoning, dates verified, public-source searches attempted, and app evidence detail where needed. Let editors promote a route to Walked only after its actual walkthrough is documented. Keep the worksheet reusable by another checker.

## File encoding

Use the user's document preferences; in a Homer project, attendee guides and worksheets use the Homer encoding, UTF-8 with a byte order mark and CRLF. Keep SKILL.md UTF-8 without BOM, with frontmatter starting at the first byte. Do not add a second title block when there is already an H1.
